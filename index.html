About

I am a cybersecurity researcher at the Cyber Resilience Laboratory, Department of Information Science, Nara Institute of Science and Technology (NAIST), Japan. My work specializes in security engineering, vulnerability assessment, and penetration testing, informed by experience across industry and academia.

I previously served as a Teaching Assistant for Network Security and Cyber Defense at Carnegie Mellon University, supporting instruction in secure systems design and adversarial thinking. My research mission is to strengthen cyber resilience in resource-constrained environments through deployable security frameworks, empirical evaluations, and practical policy recommendations.
